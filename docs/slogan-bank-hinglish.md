# ADrivera Hinglish Slogan Bank

This document archives founder-approved brand slogans, taglines, and messaging angles for future social media campaigns, print collateral, video variations, and creative testing. These lines are maintained for reference and are not currently rendered on the live static website.

---

## 1. Growth & Expansion Messaging (Established Businesses)

### G2
> "Customers aa rahe hain? Sochiye, aur kitne log aapke business ke baare mein jaan sakte hain."
- **Use Case:** Retargeting campaigns, business expansion announcements, established local brand reels.
- **Context:** Targets business owners with healthy existing demand who have not yet reached market saturation in Bhopal.

### G3
> "Achha product aapko customers deta hai. Pehchaan aapke brand ko alag bana sakti hai."
- **Use Case:** Carousel ad copy, brand positioning posts, educational content for local manufacturers and retailers.
- **Context:** Distinguishes between direct transactional utility and long-term brand equity.

### G5
> "Jab log aapke brand ko pehchaanne lagte hain, tab aap sirf ek option nahi, unki preferred choice banne ki direction mein badhte hain."
- **Use Case:** Founder LinkedIn thought leadership, long-form case study summaries, B2B sales presentations.
- **Context:** Articulates the strategic progression from discovery to brand preference.

## 2. Short Taglines & Social Ad Callouts

### L1
> "Aapka business. Aapki pehchaan. Aapka brand."
- **Format:** 3-beat cadence.
- **Use Case:** Ad visual header, story end-card, print collateral headline.

### L2
> "Right audience. Better visibility. Smarter advertising."
- **Format:** English performance summary.
- **Use Case:** Search ad descriptions, business card back, pitch decks.

### L4
> "Aapke business ko bhi online pehchaan milni chahiye."
- **Format:** Empathy & parity statement.
- **Use Case:** Small business awareness posts, local market reels.

### L5
> "Views se lekar enquiries tak, advertising ko strategy ke saath chalaiye."
- **Format:** Strategic discipline reminder.
- **Use Case:** Performance marketing carousels, monthly reporting commentary.

### L7
> "Achha business hai? Ab duniya ko pata bhi chalne dijiye."
- **Format:** Conversational prompt.
- **Use Case:** Short-form video intro hook, conversational WhatsApp outreach.

---

## 3. Brand Tone & Language Guidelines

1. **Register:** Respectful, consultative, and clear. Always use "Aap" (never "tum" or "tu").
2. **Technical Terms:** Keep core digital advertising terms in standard English (Meta Ads, Feed, Reels, WhatsApp, CPC, Reach, Impressions, Budget).
3. **Integrity:** Never introduce fabricated claims, "guaranteed sales", "100%", or "sure-shot" promises in any marketing asset.
