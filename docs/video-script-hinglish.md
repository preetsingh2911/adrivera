# ADrivera Video Ad Script & Creative Framework (Hinglish)

This document contains the founder-approved video-only creative combination for 9:16 short-form video ads (Instagram Reels & Facebook Reels). It is archived for video production use and is not imported by the website application.

---

## 1. 30-Second Reel Creative Concept

### Overview
- **Format:** 9:16 Vertical Video (Reels / Stories)
- **Target Audience:** Local business owners, retail founders, clinic operators, and hospitality managers in Bhopal.
- **Tone:** Calm, observant, confident, professional. No exaggerated sound effects or gimmicky visuals.

---

## 2. Core Script Sequence

### Segment 1: The Hook (0:00 - 0:05)
- **Visual:** Clean camera movement through a busy Bhopal storefront or cafe counter. The owner is busy managing stock or talking with a customer.
- **Voiceover (Hinglish):**
  > "Aapka business achha hai. Par kya sahi logon ko uske baare mein pata hai?"
- **On-Screen Text (Inter / Clean sans):**
  Aapka business achha hai. Par kya sahi logon ko pata hai?

### Segment 2: The Brand Message (0:05 - 0:18)
- **Visual:** Split screen or quick cut showing a customer scrolling their smartphone feed at home or work, pausing on a well-designed 4:5 sponsored ad of the same business.
- **Voiceover (Hinglish):**
  > "Business achha hona zaroori hai, par use ek pehchaan dilana bhi zaroori hai."
- **Supporting Narrative:**
  > "Meta Ads ke zariye aap Bhopal mein un logon tak pahunch sakte hain jo aapke products aur services dhund rahe hain."
- **On-Screen Text:**
  Sahi Audience • Consistent Visibility • Real Enquiries

### Segment 3: The Clear Starter Call-to-Action (0:18 - 0:30)
- **Visual:** Clean flat graphic showing the transparent ₹5,000 package breakdown: ₹3,000 Meta Ad Spend + ₹2,000 ADrivera Service Fee. Simple WhatsApp chat interface opens.
- **Voiceover (Hinglish):**
  > "Pehli baar try kar rahe hain? Sirf ₹5,000 per month se shuru kijiye. Results dekhiye, phir apne business ko next level par le jaane ka faisla kijiye."
- **End-Card Graphic:**
  - **Brand:** ADrivera | Meta Ads Management in Bhopal
  - **Direct Action:** "Send WhatsApp Message"
  - **Disclaimer (Small, legibly placed):** *Commercial terms apply. Advertising spend included. Results depend on market demand and offer quality.*
