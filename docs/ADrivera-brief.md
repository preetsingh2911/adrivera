# ADrivera — Complete Website Strategy, Design & Development Brief

## 1. Project Overview

**Brand name:** ADrivera  
**Business type:** Meta Ads and Performance Marketing Agency  
**Initial target market:** Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh, India  
**Primary platforms:** Facebook and Instagram, with WhatsApp as a potential destination for customer enquiries  
**Business model:** All-inclusive monthly advertising packages, combining agency services and the client's allocated Meta advertising spend into one transparent price.

### The objective

Build a premium, modern, mobile-first website for ADrivera that positions the agency as a credible advertising and growth partner for businesses in Bhopal.

The website must convince business owners that professional advertising is accessible, understandable, and worth testing, even if they have never run paid advertisements before.

The website should not look like a cheap local marketing agency template. It should feel like a modern performance marketing company with strong visual identity, professional execution, data-informed decisions, and transparent communication.

The desired customer reaction is:

"I understand what these people do, I understand exactly what I pay, I can start with a manageable amount, and I can evaluate their work before committing to a larger package."

### Primary business goals

1. Acquire genuine business enquiries through the website.
2. Convert visitors into WhatsApp conversations and prospective clients.
3. Make the ₹5,000 Starter plan an attractive entry point.
4. Encourage customers to upgrade when they need more advanced advertising services.
5. Establish credibility through campaign experience, real data, transparent pricing, and genuine customer case studies.
6. Build ADrivera into a recognizable agency brand that can eventually serve businesses beyond Bhopal.

## 2. Brand Positioning

ADrivera should not position itself as merely a company that runs Facebook and Instagram ads.

The broader positioning is:

**ADrivera helps businesses become more visible, attract relevant audiences, generate enquiries, and build a recognizable brand through strategic advertising.**

The agency understands that advertising serves two related purposes:

- Performance: helping businesses generate enquiries, leads, sales, and measurable customer actions.
- Brand building: helping businesses increase recognition, familiarity, and perceived value among relevant audiences.

Both matter. Some businesses need immediate enquiries, while others want to establish their name in the market over time.

### Core brand promise

"Professional advertising, transparent pricing, and decisions guided by campaign data."

### Key differentiators

- Practical experience running Meta Ads.
- Knowledge of local advertising opportunities in Bhopal.
- Audience, location, and campaign-objective selection based on the business's goals.
- Affordable, clearly defined monthly packages.
- Advertising spend included in the displayed package price.
- AI-assisted creative production in eligible packages.
- Ongoing campaign monitoring and optimization.
- Honest reporting without guaranteed-sales claims.
- A low-commitment starting option for businesses that want to test advertising.

### Brand personality

ADrivera should feel:

- Premium, not overpriced or inaccessible.
- Intelligent, not full of technical jargon.
- Confident, not exaggerated.
- Modern, not futuristic to the point of being confusing.
- Transparent, not salesy or manipulative.
- Results-oriented, without guaranteeing outcomes outside the agency's control.

## 5. Website Design Direction

### Overall visual style

Build a premium, editorial-style performance marketing website with strong typography, generous whitespace, carefully controlled motion, and a sophisticated visual hierarchy.

Avoid the generic appearance of inexpensive agency templates.

Do not overuse gradients, glowing borders, glassmorphism, floating decorative elements, excessive rounded cards, or random dashboard illustrations.

The design should look intentionally art-directed.

### Recommended color palette

- Primary background: warm off-white, approximately #F8F9FC.
- Primary text: deep navy, approximately #101A35.
- Brand accent: vivid electric blue, approximately #285BFF.
- Secondary accent: subtle blue-gray, approximately #E9EEFA.
- Supporting text: muted slate, approximately #68738A.
- Positive metrics: restrained green, approximately #16834A.
- Borders: light gray-blue, approximately #E1E6F0.
- Dark sections: deep navy, approximately #0B1530.

Use these as initial design tokens and refine them consistently throughout the website.

Blue should represent the brand's connection to technology, advertising, precision, and trust. Use accent colors sparingly.

The Starter, Growth, and Premium plans can use restrained visual distinctions, but all three should look like part of the same brand.

### Typography

Use a modern, highly readable sans-serif typeface.

Suitable options include Inter, Manrope, or Plus Jakarta Sans.

Typography should have:

- Large, confident headlines.
- Short paragraphs with comfortable line spacing.
- Clear section headings.
- Strong numeric emphasis for prices and verified campaign statistics.
- Excellent readability on mobile devices.

Avoid excessively condensed display fonts for body content.

### Imagery and graphics

Prefer custom, meaningful visuals over generic stock photographs.

Useful visual elements include:

- Authentic campaign performance screenshots, with private information removed.
- Original creative examples for different business categories.
- Carefully designed metric visualizations.
- Short examples of campaign objectives and advertising formats.
- Subtle interface-inspired visuals showing the advertising workflow.

Do not fabricate dashboards, client logos, testimonials, or campaign results.

If real evidence is not yet available, use clearly illustrative sample graphics rather than presenting invented data as actual performance.

## 6. Mobile-First Experience

Mobile is the primary design target. The majority of prospective local clients are likely to encounter the agency through a smartphone, social media, or WhatsApp.

Design for a narrow mobile screen first, then expand the layout for tablets and desktops.

### Mobile requirements

- No horizontal scrolling.
- No clipped headings or overlapping components.
- Readable body text, generally around 16px.
- Buttons with comfortable touch targets, preferably at least 44px high.
- Pricing cards that stack vertically.
- Clear spacing between sections.
- Lightweight imagery and optimized assets.
- Smooth scrolling and restrained animations.
- Fast loading on ordinary mobile connections.
- Proper handling of narrow screens, not just desktop layouts compressed into a phone.

### Mobile navigation

Use a simple header with:

- ADrivera logo or wordmark.
- A compact navigation menu.
- A prominent contact or enquiry action.

The mobile navigation should not overwhelm the screen.

Consider a discreet sticky bottom CTA such as "Get Started" or "WhatsApp Us", but ensure it does not cover content or interfere with accessibility.

### Smooth interactions

Use subtle animations for:

- Headline and section entrances.
- Metric counters only when they add value and display genuine data.
- Small hover or press states.
- Pricing-card interactions.
- Navigation transitions.

Animations should feel immediate and polished. Avoid long loading animations, scroll hijacking, heavy parallax, and unnecessary movement.

Respect reduced-motion preferences.

The website should remain fully usable if animations fail or are disabled.

## 13. Final Creative Direction

The finished website should feel like a premium performance marketing agency designed for ambitious business owners.

It must combine three things:

**Premium presentation:** strong typography, polished spacing, intentional visual design, and smooth mobile interactions.

**Commercial clarity:** understandable services, transparent all-inclusive pricing, and a simple path to enquire.

**Credibility:** realistic claims, verifiable campaign data, clear responsibilities, and honest reporting.

The website should make a small business owner feel that professional advertising is accessible without making the agency look inexpensive or inexperienced.

The ₹5,000 Starter plan is the entry point. Growth offers more creative testing and optimization. Premium and Custom provide a closer, more involved advertising partnership.

Above all, ADrivera should communicate that advertising is not merely about getting views. It is about reaching relevant audiences, learning from campaign performance, generating opportunities, and helping businesses build lasting recognition.
